Opeyemi Akin Bamidele

First-Class civil engineer from LAUTECH, Nigeria. My research spans machine learning for FRP-strengthened beams, structural health monitoring and fatigue, sustainable soil and concrete materials, and climate-driven building performance in Lagos. The question behind all of it: how do we make what already exists last longer, with less testing, less material and less carbon?

Where to start

Two published papers and the conference paper presented in November.

Published2026 · Discover Sustainability

Resource-Efficient Machine Learning for Structural Assessment and Life-Extension of FRP-Strengthened RC Beams

Trained on 624 specimens, the models predict how a strengthened beam fails and what load it carries, and a staged feature-elimination pipeline shows that two of twelve inputs can be dropped without significant loss.

Published2026 · Buildings

Cooling Degree Day Trends and Their Implications for Building Thermal Design and Thermal Fatigue Loading in Lagos

Thirty-five years of Lagos climate records show a significant rise in cooling demand and a regime shift in 2015, set against a building code that still references 1990s data.
